Disclaimer

Plain and honest, not a legal document.

Conge is not a company, and this page is not a real legal document — Conge is a small, independent, unincorporated project, and nothing here should be read as a formal terms-of-service or legal disclaimer in the way a real business would write one. It's just a plain, honest statement of what Conge is and isn't.

Read-only, no custody

Conge never requests wallet access, a signature, or a transaction of any kind. It cannot move, hold, or custody your funds, because it never has access to them in the first place — it only reads public on-chain and block-explorer data.

Not financial advice

Nothing Conge shows — a risk level, a finding, a price, anything — is financial, investment, legal, or tax advice, and none of it is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any token. Always do your own research and, if needed, talk to a qualified professional before making a financial decision.

Automated checks can miss scams

Every check Conge runs is a fast, transparent, rule-based read of public data — not an audit, and not a guarantee. A token can pass every check here and still be a scam; a token can also trigger a high-risk finding and be completely legitimate. See how it works for exactly what each check can and cannot detect.

Not affiliated with Robinhood

Conge is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or otherwise connected to Robinhood Markets, Inc. or any of its affiliates. "Robinhood Chain" is the name of the network Conge currently scans; that name and the chain itself belong to their own operator, not to this project.

Verify independently

Before making any decision, verify token information yourself on the block explorer, and don't rely on Conge — or any single tool — as your only source of truth.
